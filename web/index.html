What GENIE does here

  • The three engines run as GENIE's Action, Strategy and Memory roles on Claude, not on Gemini, ChatGPT or NotebookLM themselves.
  • Timers, the Keep-style list and the journal log work for real inside this page.
  • This page cannot reach your calendar, email, location or the web. Action drafts those items for you to add.
  • "Hey GENIE" is heard only while this page is open and your browser allows the microphone. Typing it always works.
